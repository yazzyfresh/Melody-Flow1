# Melody-Flow
# Problem Statement
Music listeners often discover artists, albums, and songs across different platforms but have difficulty keeping track of the music they want to explore, remember, and organize. Streaming services are primarily designed for listening to music rather than maintaining a personal catalog of music discoveries.
Melody Flow will provide a centralized personal music catalog where users can browse artists, albums, and songs, save music they discover, organize their personal music collection, and create playlists. Users will also be able to listen to short preview clips and track music they want to explore.
Administrators will be able to manage the music catalog and user accounts.
The application will focus on cataloging, discovering, and organizing music information rather than providing full-length music streaming.

# Business Justification
Melody Flow will provide users with a centralized place to keep track of music they discover and organize their personal music collection. Instead of functioning as a full music streaming service, the platform will focus on music catalog management, personal organization, and music discovery through short preview clips.
The project will demonstrate how a full-stack application can manage related data such as users, artists, albums, songs, personal music catalogs, and playlists.
The project will also demonstrate the required technologies from the capstone, including React, Spring Boot, MySQL, REST APIs, authentication, and AWS deployment.
